# ARCANUM — SOL SECTOR CATALOG
## Real Stellar Cartography & Conspiracy Species Registry

This document catalogs real star systems in the Sol neighbourhood with their confirmed/candidate celestial bodies, and maps conspiracy-theory alien species to their **lore-accurate** homeworlds — no relocations, no invented planets. Systems without conspiracy associations are listed as-is for future worldbuilding use.

---

## HOW TO READ THIS CATALOG

- **Confirmed exoplanets** = detected and verified through peer review
- **Candidate/Unconfirmed** = detected but disputed or awaiting verification
- **Debris disk** = ring of dust/rock indicating possible planet formation
- **HZ** = Habitable Zone (where liquid water could theoretically exist on a rocky surface)
- Distances as of latest Gaia/Hipparcos parallax measurements
- Conspiracy species listed in **[LORE]** blocks — placed ONLY where the source material puts them

**[LORE] ANNUNAKI — Nibiru**
**Source:** Zecharia Sitchin, *The 12th Planet* (1976); expanded by David Icke (1990s)
**Claimed Location:** Nibiru — a rogue planet within our own solar system on a highly elliptical ~3,600-year orbit that brings it through the inner solar system periodically. Sitchin claimed it was the "12th planet" (counting Sun and Moon in Sumerian fashion).
**Species Description:** Advanced humanoid extraterrestrials. Tall (per Sumerian depictions). Came to Earth ~500,000 years ago to mine gold. Genetically engineered Homo sapiens from Homo erectus stock as a labour force. Departed ~500 BCE. Sitchin's Annunaki are humanoid; Icke later merged them with the Reptilian conspiracy, claiming they are reptilian shapeshifters.
**Real Astronomy Note:** No planet matching Nibiru's description has been found. NASA's WISE infrared telescope survey (2014) ruled out any Saturn-sized or larger body within 10,000 AU and any Jupiter-sized body within 26,000 AU. The hypothetical "Planet Nine" (if it exists) would be far smaller and on a very different orbit than Sitchin's claims.
**Story Decision Needed:** Is Nibiru real in the ARCANUM setting? If so, where? Or are the Annunaki from elsewhere and Nibiru is a myth/mistranslation?

## ZONE 3: THE MID-RANGE (20–50 LY)

### VEGA (Alpha Lyrae) — 25.0 ly
**Star:** Single A0V blue-white main sequence
**Mass:** 2.14 M☉ | **Luminosity:** ~40× Sol | **Age:** ~455 million years (young)
**Rotation:** Very fast (~12.5 hr, ~20 km/s). Viewed nearly pole-on from Earth.

**Debris Disk:** Large, well-studied. Extends to ~800 AU. Suggests ongoing planet formation or recent collisional processing.
**Exoplanets:** One candidate reported (2021) — hot Neptune-mass object at ~0.04 AU. UNCONFIRMED.

**[LORE] LYRANS / VEGANS**
**Source:** Billy Meier contactee claims; various channelling traditions (1970s–present); Alex Collier (Andromedan contactee)
**Claimed Location:** Lyra constellation, with Vega specifically named as a major hub
**Species Description:** The "Lyrans" are described as the **original humanoid civilisation** of this region of the galaxy — tall, sometimes described with subtle feline features (cat-like eyes, prominent cheekbones). Supposedly the progenitor species from which Pleiadians, some Sirians, and (per some accounts) the genetic stock used to create humans ultimately descend. "Vegans" are described as a subgroup — refugees from the "Lyran Wars" who settled around Vega. They are said to be darker-skinned than the Pleiadian branch.
**The "Lyran Wars"** are a major mythological event in this lore — a civil war that shattered the Lyran civilisation and sent refugee groups fleeing across the galaxy.
**Real Astronomy Note:** Vega is 455 million years old. This is very young — Earth took ~4 billion years to produce intelligent life. The massive debris disk is interesting but no planets are confirmed. The system's youth makes native complex life extremely unlikely by any Earth-based model.

### ARCTURUS (Alpha Boötis) — 36.7 ly
**Star:** Single K1.5IIIpe orange giant
**Mass:** ~1.1 M☉ (current; was likely similar to Sol on main sequence) | **Radius:** ~25× Sol | **Luminosity:** ~170× Sol
**Age:** ~7.1 billion years (older than Sol, now in red giant phase)
**Proper Motion:** 2.29"/yr — one of highest of any bright star. Moving at ~122 km/s relative to Sol.

**Exoplanets:** None confirmed. Any inner planets were likely consumed during giant phase expansion. Current HZ would be at ~11 AU.
**Debris disk:** Not detected.

**[LORE] ARCTURIANS**
**Source:** Edgar Cayce (1930s references); various New Age channelling traditions; widespread in modern UFO/spiritual community
**Claimed Location:** Arcturus system
**Species Description:** Described as the "most ancient and wisest" civilisation in the galaxy. Small stature (~3–4 feet), green skin, large eyes. Highly telepathic. Operate from a "stargate" near Arcturus. Said to be 5th-dimensional beings (existing at a higher vibrational frequency). Protectors/guardians rather than interventionists.
**Real Astronomy Note:** Arcturus is a 7.1-billion-year-old red giant — it has exhausted its hydrogen fuel and expanded enormously. Any terrestrial planets it once had are gone. It's a single star with no known companions, no debris disk, and no planets. The "ancient" part of the lore loosely matches the star's real age, but the rest is pure metaphysical speculation.

**[LORE] THE GREYS / ZETA RETICULANS**
**Source:** Betty and Barney Hill abduction (1961); Marjorie Fish star map interpretation (1969); Bob Lazar Area 51 claims; Whitley Strieber, *Communion* (1987)
**Claimed Location:** Zeta Reticuli system — specifically derived from Betty Hill's claim that she was shown a star map aboard the craft, which amateur astronomer Marjorie Fish matched to the Zeta Reticuli pair in 1969.
**Species Description:** The archetypal alien. 3–4.5 feet tall, thin, grey skin, enormous black almond-shaped eyes, vestigial mouth/nose, large cranium. No visible ears. Sometimes described in two "types" — shorter worker Greys and taller "supervisor" Greys. Conduct abductions, medical examinations, genetic sampling, and reproductive experiments on humans. Associated with Roswell (1947), Area 51, and the MJ-12/Majestic conspiracy.
**Real Astronomy Note:** Both stars are genuine solar analogues — among the closest Sun-like stars. However, NO planets have been confirmed around either. Carl Sagan demonstrated in *Cosmos* (1980) that the Fish interpretation of the Hill map was statistically meaningless. The system is metal-poor relative to Sol and younger, which may work against complex life development.
**This is the single most important conspiracy species for ARCANUM** — the Greys are the default "alien" in public consciousness.

## NOTES

1. **Empty systems are real estate.** Barnard's Star, Wolf 359, Lalande 21185, Ross 128, Procyon, 61 Cygni — all within 12 ly, all without conspiracy associations. These are blank canvases for original species, colonies, or strategic installations.

2. **The richest real-planet systems have no conspiracy lore.** TRAPPIST-1 (7 planets, 40.7 ly), Teegarden's Star (3 planets, 12.5 ly), GJ 1002 (2 HZ planets, 15.8 ly) — these are the systems with the best confirmed habitability candidates, and conspiracy lore doesn't touch them.

3. **Conspiracy homeworlds are mostly astronomically hostile.** Alpha Draconis is a searing binary of giant stars. The Pleiades are 100 million years old. Arcturus has consumed its inner planets. Nibiru doesn't exist. Only Zeta Reticuli and Sirius are even vaguely plausible as habitable systems, and neither has confirmed planets.

4. **Distance creates natural tiers.** The Alpha Centaurians and Sirians are next door. The Arcturians, Lyrans, and Greys are ~25–40 ly out. The Reptilians and Pleiadians are 300–440 ly away. This distance ladder has natural implications for who Protagonist might encounter first.
